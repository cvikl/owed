  1. Identity: the order exists and belongs to this session.
    2. 
    
  2. Amount: whole cents, never more than PayPal captured.

  3. Owed by the record: overcharge, duplicate charge, cancelled before shipping.
    4. 
    
  4. Claim against the record: not received is paid only when PayPal's tracker says lost, returned or overdue.
    5. 
    
  5. Duplicates: money already paid is not paid again.

  6. Limits: one refund cap.
